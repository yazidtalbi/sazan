# Sazan 3D asset attribution

The original masterplan is the project-supplied `public/8K.png`. The WebP files
are uncropped encodings of that image, at the same aspect ratio. No generative
image model was used. Rights in the original plan remain with its owner.

## Original modular kit

`villa-kit.glb`, the vegetation geometry, hotel slabs, pool basins and pier
geometry are authored by the project's build/runtime scripts. They do not
contain third-party architecture or texture assets. The five villa variants
have flat slabs, parapets and different terrace/pergola treatments.

Meshopt and Three.js are MIT licensed; their notices are supplied by the packages.
